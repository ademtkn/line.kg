Privacy Policy

This policy explains how "Line" LLC (ОсОО «Лайн»), the operator of the Line Education website, handles information involved when you browse this site or contact us using its links.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. About this website

Website operator and contact for privacy matters: "Line" LLC (ОсОО «Лайн»). You can contact us at linekginfo@gmail.com.

Line Education is an informational website about educational programs and services. It does not offer an on-site contact form, user accounts or payment collection. We have not installed advertising trackers or visitor analytics on this website.

2. Your language preference

We save your selected language (English or Kyrgyz) in your browser’s local storage under the key linekg.language. This allows the website to remember your choice. The entry contains only the language code, en or ky, and remains until you clear the website’s stored data. Page addresses also include the selected language, so the hosting server receives that choice when you request a page. We do not use this preference for advertising or visitor profiling. You can change the language using the EN/KY buttons or remove the preference in your browser settings.

3. Technical information when you visit

To deliver the website, the hosting server receives requests from your browser. Depending on its configuration, the hosting provider may process information such as your IP address, browser details, requested page and request time for delivery, security and troubleshooting. Storage and retention depend on the hosting service and its configuration; this policy does not specify a fixed retention period for server logs.

4. Information you choose to send

If you contact Line Education by email, phone or WhatsApp, we receive the contact details and message content you choose to provide. We use that information to respond to your inquiry and discuss the information or support you request. Please share only what is needed for your question and avoid sending sensitive personal information unnecessarily.

5. Contact providers and external websites

Email, telephone and WhatsApp links open services outside this website. Gmail, WhatsApp and your telephone provider handle information according to their own policies. Our service cards also link to separate websites with their own privacy practices. Review the relevant provider’s policy when using those services.

6. Privacy questions and requests

For questions about this policy or requests concerning information you have sent to "Line" LLC (ОсОО «Лайн»), contact us at the email address below. Tell us which communication your request concerns. We will consider your request in light of the information we hold, the services used and applicable requirements.

linekginfo@gmail.com

7. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the website’s information practices change. The date above indicates the latest update.
